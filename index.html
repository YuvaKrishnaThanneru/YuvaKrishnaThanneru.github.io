Leadership

I look past the request to what the business needs next.

Some of my best work came from following a question past the original ask, and from building things other people could use.

"I don't just analyze what happened. I build the foundations to understand why it happened, and help the business decide what to do next."
01

I turned one win into the next opportunity

The amber vial work made me curious about why so many filled prescriptions were never picked up, so I dug in. Across 78M records, the analysis shows which products drive the most abandoned volume, whether that's chain-wide or tied to specific sites and stores, and what share could ship in manufacturer packaging instead of amber vials.

02

I built a framework other teams adopted

I automated weekly financial-controls emails with Python and Outlook VBA macros, organizing reports so evidence is easy to find during audits. Other teams took up the same framework, replacing a lot of manual work.

03

I bring teams to one version of the truth

I partnered with Operations and Asset Protection to standardize inventory KPI definitions, then consolidated reporting for all 15 micro-fulfillment centers into one Power BI workspace. Both teams now work from the same numbers, defined the same way.

04

I partner closely with business leaders

I work directly with site directors and senior leaders to understand what's happening beyond the numbers. Sometimes that means challenging an assumption, uncovering a problem nobody was measuring, or connecting findings across different parts of the operation. I want every conversation to move us closer to a decision, not just another report.

05

I grow the people around me

I mentor junior analysts on understanding the business behind the data, building reliable SQL, validating their results, and designing Power BI reports that answer real operational questions. I review their work, share what I've learned, and help them take ownership of their solutions. It's the same hands-on approach I developed while working as a teaching assistant at NIU.

06

I learned early that accuracy earns trust

At Citi, I supported testing, validation, and discrepancy resolution for financial systems, where getting the details right mattered. That foundation shaped how I approach analytics today—from validating underlying data to building dependable financial and operational reporting at Walgreens.
